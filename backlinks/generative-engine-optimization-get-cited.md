## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How do I get my brand cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews?

I'm Jordan Cole. I run performance marketing for B2B brands, and over the past year this has become the question clients ask me most. The short answer: AI engines cite pages that hold specific, sourced facts they can lift and credit, and they cite brands that other sites already describe the same way. Keyword density barely registers.

Here is what that looks like in practice.

1. Replace claims with numbers. "Fast implementation" gives a model nothing to quote. "Average go-live in 11 business days across 140 customers in 2025" does. Every key page should carry at least a few facts like that.

2. Put the source next to the fact. Name where the number comes from: internal data with a date range, a named study, a public dataset. Models prefer claims they can attribute.

3. Write so answers can be pulled out cleanly. Lead each section with a direct answer in one or two sentences, then support it. Use clear headings that match how buyers phrase questions.

4. Build corroboration off your own site. If review sites, industry publications, partner pages and forum threads repeat the same facts about you, models treat those facts as safe to state. One page saying it is weak. Five independent sources saying it is strong.

5. Test with real prompts. Write the questions your buyers actually ask, run them through Perplexity and ChatGPT, and record who gets cited and which page they pull from. Repeat monthly.

I did this for a B2B payroll client spending about $85,000 a month on paid search. We ran 60 buyer-style prompts, and the pattern was clear: the cited pages were the ones with quotable, sourced numbers. I wrote up the full method here if you want the details: https://siavashsed.github.io/aimarketingpro/generative-engine-optimization-get-cited/

Start with your five highest-value pages. Add real numbers, add their sources, then go earn the outside mentions that confirm them.
