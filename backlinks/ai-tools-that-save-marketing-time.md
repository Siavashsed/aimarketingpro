## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Which AI tools actually save time for marketers, and which ones end up wasting it?

After years running paid media and now consulting on AI workflows, my honest answer is that the tools that save time are usually the dull ones. They sit on top of work you already repeat every week, so there is nothing new to learn and nothing to rebuild.

The four places I see real time come back:

1. Reporting. Last March I timed myself building a weekly client report from Meta Ads Manager exports, Google Ads, and three spreadsheets. It took 94 minutes. Once I put the same exports into a Claude project with a fixed report structure, most of that time disappeared. What remained was checking the numbers and writing the commentary, which is the valuable part.

2. First drafts. Not final copy, just getting past the blank page for emails, ad copy, and briefs. You still edit, but you start at 60 percent instead of zero.

3. Call transcripts. Summaries, action items, and objections pulled from sales or client calls save a surprising amount of admin.

4. Creative variants. Producing ten headline or hook angles from one winning idea is a perfect job for AI, because you are selecting, not creating from scratch.

The tools that waste time share a few traits. They ask you to build an entirely new workflow around them. They need re-prompting from scratch every time. Or the output needs so much checking that reviewing it takes as long as doing the task yourself.

My test is simple. Pick the task you do every week and like least. Time it before and after. If you are not saving at least a third of the time including review, drop the tool.
